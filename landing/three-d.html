Three moving parts

Creative where it helps. Rigid where it counts.

01 — PROPOSES

The model reads, and asks.

It sees names, prices and stock. Never a cost, never a customer. A poisoned product description has nothing to steal.

02 — DECIDES

Five checks, three answers.

Pass, fail, or not configured. A merchant sees which protections are actually running, not a row of green ticks.

03 — SETTLES

Silence is not failure.

When a webhook never arrives it asks Razorpay what happened rather than guessing. It never charges twice.
