What a merchant has to hand over

Onboarding is a ladder, not a threshold. A merchant who shares nothing sensitive still gets an enforced boundary.

Level 1 — default

A price list and a limit

Products, stock, and the maximum discount they will tolerate. No costs, no customer data. The cap binds just as hard as a margin floor would.

Level 2 — opt in

A read-only role

SELECT on named tables, granted by their own DBA and revocable in one line. Buys real margin enforcement, live stock, and recommendations for returning shoppers.

Level 3 — refused

Their full credentials

Not offered. One breach would expose every shop's cost structure and every shopper's history. Declined rather than built.
